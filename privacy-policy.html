Privacy Policy

At Wynklo, your trust is our most important asset. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we protect it, and your choices regarding your personal data when you partner with our digital studio.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us when using our website, filling out our contact forms, or utilizing our interactive project preview wizard. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Business Details: Your business or project name, industry category, location, and specific requirements.
  • Project Features: Selections made during our onboarding wizard to help customize your interactive wireframe preview.

How We Use Your Data

The information we gather is used purely to deliver professional digital solutions and keep you informed about your projects:

  • To construct and render your custom interactive AI project preview.
  • To follow up with accurate cost estimates, project recommendations, and proposals.
  • To communicate with you regarding design revisions, staging link details, or agency news.
  • To maintain our systems' stability, monitor network analytics, and verify security protocols.

Zero Data Selling Policy

Wynklo will never sell, rent, or lease your personal data, project details, or business files to any third party. Your ideas and details stay secure with us under full confidentiality standards.

Third-Party Services

We may utilize trusted third-party APIs and services to facilitate our operations. These services handle data in accordance with their respective security protocols:

  • Analytics: Basic telemetry to monitor performance and page load speeds.
  • Hosting & Security: Servers run under verified SSL protocols with regular uptime audits.
  • Nodemailer & Emailers: Used strictly to route onboarding form inquiries directly to our secure support inbox.

Data Security

We enforce high-security standards across all platforms. Our site communicates exclusively via SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encryption, and all customer databases are restricted to verified administrators behind strict authentication protocols to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or distribution.

Your Privacy Rights

You maintain complete authority over the personal data you share with Wynklo. You may request to:

  • Access the personal information we have stored associated with your account.
  • Update or correct any inaccurate details.
  • Completely remove your contact logs and onboarding wizard features from our systems.

To initiate any of these requests, please reach out to us at contactuswynklo@gmail.com.

Policy Modifications

Wynklo reserves the right to make minor adjustments to this policy to align with industry shifts or legislation. We advise reviewing this page periodically. Continued utilization of our digital platforms will signify approval of our updated practices.
